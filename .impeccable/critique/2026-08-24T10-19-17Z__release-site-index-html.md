## Material issues

### P1. Hero blurs local records and requested online AI

The first viewport pairs Local-first with photo or description estimation before the consent and provider boundary appears. Qualify the hero immediately: manual and history logging stay local; a requested AI estimate sends only selected content after consent.

### P1. Mobile product proof arrives late and tiny

At 320px no screenshot appears in the first viewport. The four-screen gallery remains two columns, producing screens about 146px wide. Bring a legible compact Today crop into the first viewport and render workflow screens one per row or as a labeled accessible snap gallery.

### P2. Steps and screens are not paired

Descriptions live in one rail and screenshots in a separate grid. Pair Input with Today, Review with Review meal, Read with weight Analytics, and Decide with the plan recommendation using visible labels and semantic figures.

### P2. Mobile navigation disappears

Plans and core sections require thousands of pixels of scrolling. Add a compact accessible mobile route to the core sections and Plans without elevating unfinished Privacy or Terms.

### P2. Calorie blue is used as a generic website accent

Selection, focus, kickers, FAQ marks, and prose links borrow the calorie semantic. Add a neutral web interaction token and reserve calorie blue for calorie information.

## Deterministic finish findings
