## Design Specificity Verdict

Highly authored for Marco. Nutrient colors retain stable meaning, the consumed/remaining ring, adaptive review, scanner-first entry, and weight-trend treatment form a coherent training-instrument identity. Detector reported 25 font-size advisories; 10px compact nutrition and ruler metadata were intentional false positives, while three 11px values were genuine type-ramp drift.

## What's Working

- Nutrient color semantics remain consistent across Dashboard, Diary, sheets, and Analytics.
- Failure states preserve trust with shaped skeletons, card-level retries, and explicit recovery copy.
- Accessibility includes adjustable ruler actions, journal custom actions, chart summaries, and live regions.

## Priority Issues

### P0: Diary logging ignored the viewed date

Manual, search, scan, and meal-review inserts hardcoded today even when the user viewed a past Diary day. Thread the selected date through sheet state, show it in the sheet, and preserve it while editing historical meals.

### P1: Diary portion editing bypassed shared Sheet behavior

The custom overlay lacked hardware Back handling, discard protection, modal focus containment, and the M3 sheet vocabulary. Replace it with shared Sheet behavior and a private discard guard.

### P1: Weight overwrite was irreversible

Updating an existing check-in replaced its value without undo. Capture the prior value; restore it on undo, or delete a newly created row and recompute trend.

### P2: Calorie overage was hidden in consumed mode

The ring clamped at full and only exposed overage in Remaining mode. Keep a persistent over-target caption in both modes.

### P2: Analytics lacked chart explanation

Add a shared Scale / Trend / Goal legend, replace EWMA jargon with Trend, and explain adaptive reviews contextually.

## Minor Observations

- Three 11px values sat outside the type ramp.
- Chart axes did not use bundled Inter.
- Week-strip scroll positioning relied on an estimated cell width.
- Dashboard weight empty state lacked a direct Log weight action.
- Ruler and consumed/remaining controls lacked haptic feedback.
- MacroRail P/C/F labels intentionally remain white; colored progress bars carry nutrient identity.

## Questions

- Does every destructive data update offer recovery proportional to its consequence?
- Does the adaptive lock explain both what is needed and what unlocks afterward?
- Can each range change preserve one chart object and morph its geometry without stale intermediate state?
