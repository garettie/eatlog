Before you install

Straight answers.

Nutrition and weight data deserve precise language. The longer version lives in the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Does Eatlog need an account?

No. Eatlog has no login or cloud identity. The app uses an app-scoped installation token for online-service abuse limits and purchase access checks, but it is not an account.

Can I use Eatlog without AI estimates?

Yes. Manual logging, local history, food search, weight tracking, analytics, backup, export, and sharing remain available when you decline online meal estimates.

Are nutrition estimates medical advice?

No. Eatlog is for adult general wellness. Nutrition values, targets, trends, and estimates can be incomplete or wrong. Review entries and speak with a qualified health professional about medical decisions.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your local food, weight, target, photo, and adaptive history stays on the device. Paid AI and adaptive access ends when the verified entitlement expires or is revoked.

Does Eatlog sync with health platforms?

Android users can optionally connect Health Connect for Weight read and write access. Eatlog does not use HealthKit or Apple Health in v1.
