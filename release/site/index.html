FINE PRINT, NORMAL SIZE

The questions people ask before installing.

The longer, less charming versions live in the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Does Eatlog need an account?

No. Eatlog has no login or cloud identity. The app uses an app-scoped installation token for online-service abuse limits and purchase access checks, but it is not an account.

Do I need an API key to use Eatlog?

No. Manual logging, saved meals, food search, weight tracking, analytics, backup, export, and sharing work without a key or purchase. A key only enables optional direct Gemini estimates.

Is Eatlog really open source?

Yes. The app source is on GitHub under 0BSD, which lets anyone use, copy, change, and distribute it without asking. Read the code, report a bug, or propose a change. The license covers the app source, not access to Eatlog's hosted AI service.

How do I get a Gemini key?

Create one in Google AI Studio, then paste it into Profile → AI estimates. Eatlog checks it with Google before saving it. The key stays in your phone's secure storage and never goes into backups or exports.

Is My key free?

Eatlog does not charge for it. Google controls Gemini's free and paid usage, quotas, availability, and data terms for your Google project. You can see those terms before saving a key in the app.

Where does an AI estimate go?

My key sends the selected photo or text from your phone to Google. Eatlog AI sends it through Eatlog's Worker to Google. Your diary stays on the phone either way. Both routes need their own consent.

Are nutrition estimates medical advice?

No. Eatlog is for adult general wellness. Nutrition values, targets, trends, and estimates can be incomplete or wrong. Review entries and speak with a qualified health professional about medical decisions.

What does Omelette buy?

A one-time purchase lets you use Eatlog-hosted estimates without your own key, within the hosted allowance. It does not add local features. A refund or revocation can end hosted access; your local diary stays on the device.

Does Eatlog sync with health platforms?

Android users can optionally connect Health Connect for Weight read and write access. Eatlog does not use HealthKit or Apple Health in v1.
